---
title: Put the browser on the right
date: 2026-10-24T16:00:00Z
summary: In the desktop app, the Browser panel can open in its own column on the right, so the agent chat and the browser are open side by side.
tags: [new]
---

The built-in browser used to share the left side panel with the agent, so you saw one or the other. Now you can move it.

- Choose **Settings → General → Browser panel → Right**.
- The Browser icon in the rail then opens its own column on the right of the canvas. The left panel stays free for the agent, layers or assets.
- Chat with the agent on the left while it searches Pinterest, Dribbble or Google in the browser on the right.
- Drag the column's left edge to resize it. Pego remembers the width, and whether the column was open.
- The column runs the full height of the window, mirroring the side panel on the left. Its header has buttons to move the browser back to the side panel and to close it.

It works in both the docked and the floating interface. Choose **Left** to go back to the side panel.
