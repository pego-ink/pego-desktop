---
title: Export projects from Home
date: 2026-10-23T16:00:00Z
summary: Download a project, or every screen in it as PNG, SVG, HTML/CSS or React + Tailwind, straight from Home.
tags: [new]
---

Open a project's menu on Home (the **…** button or a right-click) and pick **Export**:

- **Pego file** downloads the project's design as a `.pego` file.
- **PNG @2x**, **SVG**, **HTML / CSS** and **React + Tailwind** export every screen of the project in one ZIP, with a folder per page.

No need to open the project first. Hidden layers and library pages are left out.
