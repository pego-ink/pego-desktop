---
title: The Pego browser extension
date: 2026-10-23T18:30:00Z
summary: Send any web page, an element of it, or a screenshot from Chrome to your canvas, as editable layers.
tags: [new]
version: 0.1.0-beta
---

A new Chrome extension sends what you see on the web straight into Pego:

- **Send this page as layers**: the whole page becomes editable frames, text and images, next to your design.
- **Pick an element**: point at a header, a card or a section and click it to bring just that part in.
- **Send a screenshot** of what is on screen.

Images on the page come along, even from sites that block other pages from loading them. Pair the extension once with the code in **Settings › Agent & Models › Browser extension**; it only talks to Pego on your own computer.
