---
title: Photo search uses Google in the desktop app
date: 2026-10-24T12:00:00Z
summary: In the desktop app, photo search in the Images panel and by the agent now looks on Google Images with the built-in browser. Free photos are one setting away.
tags: [improved]
---

In the desktop app, photo search now goes to Google Images by default, through Pego's built-in browser.

- **Assets → Images → Search** shows Google's pictures. Click one or drag it onto the canvas, and it is downloaded into your file.
- **The agent** searches the same way. You see the Browser panel open while it looks.
- If Google asks for a check (a captcha) or finds nothing, Pego shows free photos from Openverse instead and tells you why. Use **Open the Browser panel** to complete the check, then search again.

Pictures from the web are usually copyrighted, so each placed one keeps the page it came from. For openly licensed photos with their credit line, choose **Settings → General → Image search → Free photos**.

The built-in browser now introduces itself as a regular Chrome, so sites treat it like your usual browser.
