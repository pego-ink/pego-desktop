## Assets panel

Your components now live in one panel, grouped by page, with live thumbnails and search. Drag a component onto the canvas, or press **+** to place it. Every card works from the keyboard too: Enter shows the component, Shift+Enter inserts it.

## Save as project

**File › Save as project** turns the document you are looking at into a new project. If it wasn't saved to a file yet, the chat that built it comes along.

## Copy for AI

Select layers and press **⌘⇧C** to copy a short reference to them: the project, the file and the layers. Paste it into Claude Code or another assistant so it knows exactly what you mean.

## Claude Code works on any project

Ask Claude Code to change a project by name and it edits that project in a background tab, without switching what you see. If the project's file can't be read, it stops and tells you instead of starting from an empty design.

## Right-click does what you expect

A right-click now acts on what you selected. Right-click a frame's name above it to get that frame's full menu, and right-clicking inside a selected frame keeps it selected instead of switching to a layer inside it or clearing the selection.

The menu also ends with **Settings**, even on empty canvas: turn snapping to whole pixels on or off, or open all settings.

## Name your agents

**Settings › Agent names** picks the names on the agents' canvas cursors: Pego (the default, every agent is called Pego), Artists, Philosophers, Inventors, Mythic, Scientists, or your own ten names. Up to three agents work at once; each takes the first free name with its color and keeps it for its chat, so you can tell at a glance which agent is working where.

## Put the toolbar where you like

The tools (Move, Hand, frame, shapes, pen, text) can now sit at the bottom, top, left or right of the canvas. Right-click the toolbar to move it, or pick a side in **Settings › General › Toolbar position**. On the left or right it becomes a column, and its menus open toward the canvas.

## A floating interface

**Settings › General › Interface › Floating** turns the panels into cards that float over the canvas, like pen.dev: the tabs sit on a slim top row with a pill for undo, redo and Share, the side panel's icons line up on top of its card, and the properties get a card of their own. Close the side panel and it shrinks to a small pill of icons. Your design runs on behind the panels, and zoom to fit, zoom to selection and pasting aim at the part you can see. Docked stays the default.
