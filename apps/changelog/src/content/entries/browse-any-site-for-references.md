title: The agent browses any site for pictures and references
date: 2026-10-24T14:00:00Z
summary: In the desktop app, ask the agent to look on Pinterest, Unsplash, Dribbble, Behance or any site. It finds the pictures, studies them, and uses them.
tags: [new, improved]
---

Pego's built-in browser opens any website, and the agent now makes better use of it.

- **Name a site:** "find inspiration for a coffee shop landing page on Dribbble", or "get a mountain photo from Unsplash". The agent opens that site's search in the built-in browser and lists what it finds.
- **Full size:** pictures come at the largest size the page offers, not as thumbnails (Pinterest originals, 2000 px from Unsplash, 1200 px Dribbble shots). Avatars and the site's own icons are left out.
- **The agent looks closely:** it can open up to four pictures at a time and study them: layout, hierarchy, type, color and spacing. It then makes its own design from those ideas instead of copying.
- **Credit and licenses:** the agent tells you where each picture came from. Unsplash and Pexels photos are free to use, Unsplash+ photos are marked as paid, and work from Pinterest, Dribbble or Behance belongs to its authors.

Pictures the agent places on the canvas are downloaded into your file, so they keep working offline.
