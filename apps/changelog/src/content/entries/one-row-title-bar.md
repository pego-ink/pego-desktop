---
title: A tidier title bar on Linux and Windows
date: 2026-10-24T11:00:00Z
summary: On Linux and Windows the desktop app now has one clean top row, your tabs with the window buttons beside them, instead of a title bar, a menu bar and the tabs.
tags: [improved]
---

On Linux and Windows the desktop app stacked three bars: the system title bar, a File / Edit / View / Window menu bar, and the tab row. Now there is one.

- Your tabs are the title bar. Drag the empty space to move the window, and double-click it to maximize.
- Minimize, maximize and close sit at the right end, in the colors of your light or dark theme.
- The menu moved behind the **☰** button at the left of the row.
- Ctrl+W still closes the current tab.

Prefer your desktop's own title bar? Choose **Settings → General → Title bar → System** and restart Pego.
