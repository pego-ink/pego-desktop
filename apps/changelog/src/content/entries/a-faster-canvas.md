## Right-click menu

Right-click a layer for the actions you reach for most.

- Copy as SVG, PNG, React + Tailwind, HTML/CSS or JSON.
- Paste here, or Paste to replace the selection (⌘⇧R).
- Add or remove flex layout (⇧A, ⇧⌥A).
- Flip horizontal or vertical (⇧H, ⇧V). Flip now works on any layer.
- Hide or show a layer, and turn Clip content on or off.

## Resizing

A selection shows four corner handles. Drag an edge anywhere along its length to resize from that side.

## Watching the agent work

The layers the agent is working on show a soft loading shimmer and a single "Generating" label. Its cursor on the canvas now just says "Pego".
