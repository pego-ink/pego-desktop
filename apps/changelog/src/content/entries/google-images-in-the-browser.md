---
title: The agent can find pictures on Google
date: 2026-10-24T10:00:00Z
summary: In the desktop app, ask for images "from Google" and the agent searches Google Images in the built-in browser and places the photos on your canvas.
tags: [new, improved]
---

The agent used to find photos only in Openverse, a library of free-to-use images. Now, in the desktop app, it can also look on Google.

- Ask for it in your own words, for example "find a photo of forest fire smoke on Google". The agent opens Google Images in the built-in browser, picks a fitting photo, and places it on the canvas at full size.
- If Google asks for a check or for cookie consent, the agent tells you. Complete it in the Browser panel and ask again.
- Photos from the web are usually copyrighted. The agent tells you where each one came from, and the source page stays on the layer.

Without a request for Google, the agent still picks free-to-use photos from Openverse, with their credit line.

## Web images that show up

Pictures the agent places from any website are now downloaded into your file. Images from sites that block other websites no longer stay empty on the canvas, and your design keeps working offline.
