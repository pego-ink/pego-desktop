---
title: Images from the web
date: 2026-10-23T18:00:00Z
summary: Bring any image from the web into your design by its link, or copy it on a site and paste it on the canvas.
tags: [new]
version: 0.1.0-beta
---

- **Assets › Images › From URL**: paste the link of an image on the web and press **Place**.
- **Paste from any site**: right-click an image in your browser, choose **Copy image** (or copy its link), then paste on the canvas.

The image is downloaded into your file, like an image you drop in. Many sites don't let other pages load their images; then the Pego bridge fetches it for you. It only fetches public https images, never addresses on your own network.
