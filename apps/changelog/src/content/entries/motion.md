Designs can now move. Select a layer and open **Animation** in the properties panel.

- Pick an effect: fade in, slide in from any side, scale in, pop, or grow up / grow right for bars and charts.
- Set a delay and a duration, and choose the easing (ease out, ease in-out, linear or spring).
- Press **Play** to watch the whole page come in on the canvas. While you edit, everything stays where you designed it.

## One by one

Select a row, a list or a chart and press **One by one**. Every item gets the same entrance, each a little after the one before, so steps, cards and bars come in in turn.

## Counting numbers

Turn on **Count numbers up** on a text such as "70%" or "± 6 bulan", and its numbers count up from zero while it plays. Decimals and leading zeros stay as you wrote them.

## In your code

Export HTML or React and the motion comes with it: CSS animations with the same timing as the canvas, and a small script that counts the numbers up in HTML. Visitors who prefer reduced motion see the design as is.

The agent knows about motion too: ask it to animate a section, and it sets the entrances for you.
