title: A new instrument-panel style
date: 2026-10-23T17:00:00Z
summary: Ask the agent for a design that looks like a portable synthesizer, with grey panels, numbered keys, knobs, small screens and one orange, in light or dark.
tags: [new]
---

- A new style inspired by portable synthesizers: grey panels, numbered keys, knobs, small black screens with dot-matrix numbers, labels printed in small capitals and a single orange for the one control that matters.
- It comes in a light version, like brushed aluminium, and a dark version for a black finish. Social posts use the dark one unless you ask otherwise.
- It works for app dashboards, landing pages and social posts, and for any product, not only music gear.
- Ask for it in your own words, for example "a dashboard with a synth UI" or "desain seperti synthesizer", or name Field Instrument as the style.
