title: Bring back an earlier message with ↑
date: 2026-10-24T09:00:00Z
summary: In the agent chat, press ↑ to bring back what you sent before, edit it and send it again. ↓ returns to your draft.
tags: [improved]
---

The agent chat now remembers what you sent, like a terminal.

- In an empty box, or with the cursor at the very start, press **↑** to bring back your last message. Press it again for older ones.
- **↓** steps back to newer messages and, past the newest, to the draft you were writing.
- Edit the message you brought back and press Enter to send it as a new one. Once you edit it, the arrow keys move the cursor again.

Messages you typed in this window come back with their @ mentions. Older ones, from before a reload, come back as text.
