The editor has a new skin, in light and dark. The panels and bars use quiet, grape-tinted neutrals so your canvas stands out, and color shows up only when it means something.

## Grape is the agent, bubblegum is you

The agent's plans, steps, tool calls and its "Designing…" bar are grape, and so are primary buttons such as Share. Your own selection, its handles and size label, the selected layer and your chat messages are bubblegum, so you can tell at a glance what you did and what the agent did. A question from the agent shows up in an amber card while the run waits for you.

## Easier to read

Interface text is set in Geist, and values such as positions, sizes, colors, ids and tool names are in JetBrains Mono. Section labels like Plan, Pages and Layers are small mono capitals.

## Tools on the left

The canvas tools now float on the left edge by default, next to your panels, so they no longer cover the bottom of your frames. You can still move them to another edge in Settings › General › Toolbar position, or by right-clicking the toolbar.
